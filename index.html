Circled & Noted

tone — Intimate · a notebook only you write in

Everything here is drawn by hand, slowly, for one reader. A badge is you circling something in red pen — and crossing it out when it's done. A tooltip is a margin note or a sticky note slapped onto the page. Hover a doodle and its lines start to boil.
